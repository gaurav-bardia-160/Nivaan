Main meaning of folders:

backend/offline/
  Future offline knowledge-building pipeline: crawl official sources, clean/chunk text, structure entries, verify entries, and build an index.

backend/modules/
  Future online user-serving modules: OCR, privacy masking, routing, retrieval, flagging, explanation, agent tools, translation, and voice.

backend/data/
  Future knowledge storage: source list, raw crawled pages, cleaned chunks, reviewed entries, verified entries, and vector index.

frontend/
  Future website UI: question box, upload area later, language selector, result rendering.

samples/
  Fake sample contracts and fake documents only. No real personal/confidential documents.

docs/
  Architecture, build order, contract planning, and privacy notes.

evaluation/
  Future test results, user-testing results, timings, feedback, and accuracy notes.

presentation/
  Future slides, demo script, privacy slide, and backup demo notes.

tests/
  Future automated tests for contracts, retrieval, privacy masking, OCR, flagger, and integration.
