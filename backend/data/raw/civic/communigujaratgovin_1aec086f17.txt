Programmes & Schemes
- Nagar Seva Sadan Sahay” Scheme was launched by Urban Development and Urban Housing Department's GR dated 17/11/2009, which will support those municipalities that need to form a new building or to Renovate the existing old building.
- According to The GR of 23/8/2011, the Grant is determined according to the Class of municipality.
- As per the GR of 10/12/2013, increase the Grant under following scheme.
| Class of Municipalities | Not own office building (or) For more than 30 years old office building | 
|---|---|
| A & B class | Rs. 200 lacs | 
| C & D class | Rs. 100 lacs | 
For repairs / improvements of office building: -
- As per the GR of 10/12/2013, Grant allotted as per requirement for repair / improvement of municipal building.
