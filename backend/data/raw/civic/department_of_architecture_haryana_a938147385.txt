TITLE: Vision & Mission | Department Of Architecture Haryana | India

Vision & Mission
VISION
To ensure functional, economical, energy efficient, eco-friendly and sustainable built environment with Green Technologies.
MISSION
Designing capacity buildings of green technology to achieve environmental and user friendly built environment.
To ensure aesthetical living environment with planned and regulated development for humanity
To ensure rational use of available land and water resources in every campus by Sustainable development.
