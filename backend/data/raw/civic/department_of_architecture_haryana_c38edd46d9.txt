Welcome To Department Of Architecture Haryana
The Department of Architecture, Haryana, is a State Government Organization with vast and varied experience of more than 54 years. The Department has a key role in the designing and development of infrastructure in the State of Haryana. It renders architectural services to all State Government Departments/ Boards/Corporations/Autonomous Bodies/ Universities.The Department endeavours to evolve innovative/user friendly building designs within the parameters of various rules/regulations/Codal provisions governing the building designs. The Department of Architecture also coordinates with the user department as well as the different Engineering departments i.e. Public Health, Electrical, Structure etc. at planning stage and at field level
