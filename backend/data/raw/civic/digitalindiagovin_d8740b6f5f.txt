Inauguration of the myScheme Portal in Uttarakhand: A Step Towards Digital Governance
The myScheme Portal was inaugurated on December 10, 2025, in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, by the Hon’ble Chief Minister of Uttarakhand, Shri Pushkar Singh Dhami. The inauguration marked an important step in the State’s digital governance initiatives, aimed at strengthening citizen access to government welfare schemes.
The myScheme Portal is a unified, user-centric digital platform that enables citizens to easily search, identify, and discover government schemes they are eligible for across multiple sectors. Currently featuring over 400+ state government schemes, the platform is designed to ensure transparency, efficiency, and inclusivity in the delivery of scheme benefits. The event highlighted the Government’s commitment to leveraging technology for last-mile delivery and empowering citizens through simplified access to welfare benefits
