Joint Integrated Programme in Management Admission Test (JIPMAT)
Introduction
The Ministry of Education, Government of India (GoI), has established National Testing Agency (NTA) as an independent, autonomous and self-sustained premier testing organization under Society Registration Act 1860 for conducting efficient, transparent and international standards tests in order to assess the competency of candidates for admission to premier higher education institutions.
National Testing Agency has been entrusted to conduct Joint Integrated Programme in Management Admission Test (JIPMAT) – 2026 for admission to Five Year Integrated Programme in Management at Indian Institute of Management - Bodh Gaya and Indian Institute of Management - Jammu.
