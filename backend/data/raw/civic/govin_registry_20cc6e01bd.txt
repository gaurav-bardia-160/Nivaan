TITLE: GOV.IN Registry - Procedure

For ease of user onboarding Gov.in Registry has now been integrated with NIC Single Sign-On Platform (Parichay). Now, users will be authenticated through Parichay(SSO)
Login at registry.gov.in and generate Domain Name cancellation request letter.
Print the Letter without any modification on pre-printed official letterhead of Signing Authority and upload the scan copy in PDF
format on the Registry Site. The letter must be signed by the owner of the Domain (Administrative/Organisational contact).
Domain registrar will verify your request and cancel the requested domain name and intimate the registered user through e-mail.
The status of the domain name Cancellation request can be verified by clicking check status link at registry.gov.in
Note:
Domain submitted for cancellation takes 35 days for final deletion. For 30 days after submission for cancellation the status would be shown as "Pending Delete Status" and then for next 5 days Status would be "Delete on Hold Status" at https://registry.gov.in/domain_whois.php
