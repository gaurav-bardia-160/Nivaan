S3waas
S3WaaS (Secure, Scalable, and Accessible Websites as a Service) is a cloud-based platform designed to develop secure, scalable, and accessible websites for government organizations. It enables government entities to easily create, customize, and manage website content through a variety of standardized templates, thereby ensuring a strong and consistent online presence.
The Government has developed 52 websites of Haryana for various Departments, Boards, and Corporations, along with 22 District websites, on the Secure, Scalable, and Sugamya Website as a Service (S3WaaS) platform of NIC.
