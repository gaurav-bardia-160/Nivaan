About Department
Haryana has always been a land of sportspersons & our players have always made a special contribution to the sports achievements of India. Almost one-third of the medals won by the nation in almost all international competitions are won by the Haryanvis. To promote this sports culture further, the Sports Department, Haryana is working hard for:
Popularization of Sports.
Development of Sports Infrastructure.
Identification and development of talented athletes from an early age.
Creating income and employment opportunities for career sportspersons and implementing youth development programs.
To harness sports’ community-building potential and channel youth to constructive pursuits.
