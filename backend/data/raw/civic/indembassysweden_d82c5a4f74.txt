Fees for OCI Services
Important information before payment for the service OCI.
Applicants are requested to pay the fee for the service(s) requested, through online bank transfer to given below account number.
It is mandatory to give the following details in the Message box (Meddelande box) of the transaction sheet.
- Service(s) requested for
- Name of the applicant (s)
- Mobile/Telephone number
(Embassy does not accept cash/cheque at present)
Useful Information Points:
Embassy accepts all fees in SEK only.
Fee once paid are non-refundable.

Important Information for Applicants
- Applicants are advised to read the instructions carefully on the online OCI portal before submitting their application.
- It is mandatory to upload all relevant/supporting documents while completing the online application. Failure to upload the required documents may delay the processing of the application.
- Applicants must also submit copies of the uploaded documents along with the printed/hard copy of the application at the Embassy.
- Applicants may visit the OCI portal for the latest guidelines and further information:OCI Online Services Portal (https://ociservices.gov.in/)
- Normal processing time for application is 6-8 weeks.
- Track status of your application: https://ociservices.gov.in/statusEnqury
