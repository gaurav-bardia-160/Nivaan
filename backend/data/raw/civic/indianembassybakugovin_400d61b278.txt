TITLE: Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) Scheme | Embassy of India, Baku, Azerbaijan

Embassy of India 
Baku 
***** 
NOTICE 
The Government of India has launched electronic Overseas Citizen of India (e-OCI) Cards in lieu of physical OCI cards for the new applicants and existing OCI card holding physical booklets. 
The following are the useful points with regard to e-OCI cards : 
a) e-OCI card has been launched on 30.06.2026 for the new applicants and existing OCI card holding physical booklets. 
b) The new e-OCI card incorporates enhanced security features and has been integrated with all the Immigration Check Posts (ICPs). 
c) The holder of e-OCI card will need not carry the physical copy of the OCI card at the time of Immigration Clearance and for availing other services. 
d) The e-OCI card can be downloaded by login into OCI portal (https://ociservices.gov.in ) and through the official ‘Indian Visa Su Swagatam” mobile app. 
e) The physical booklets shall continue to remain valid and shall be accepted for immigration and other associated services. 
It is informed that now onwards, only digital OCI card (e-OCI) will be issued to all new OCI applicants as well as existing physical booklet OCI Card holders.
