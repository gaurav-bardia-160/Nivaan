Entry Visa
Purpose of Visit
1)
Afghan Nationals (Artists/Cultural
Personalities/Academicians/ Professionals) intending to
visit India to participate in cultural events/ activities
sponsored by reputed institutions in India for short
duration without remuneration.
2)
Minor children accompanying a parent (only Afghan
Nationals), who has come for medical treatment on a Medical
Visa.
3)
Minor child/ children from previous marriages of an Afghan
National who is presently married to an Indian
Citizen/Person of Indian Origin/OCI cardholder.
4)
Afghan Nationals, who own property in the country subject to
the condition that he/she must be having a documentary proof
of permission from Reserve Bank of India for the purchase
of/holding the property in India and the document should
show registration of the particular property with the
registration authority concerned.
5)
Ordinary passport holding dependents of Diplomats/Officials
assigned to India (including in the UN and Non-UN
International Organizations).
6)
Either or both Afghan parents/legal guardians (in the
absence of parents) of a minor child less than 18 years of
age coming on Student Visa.
7)
Dependent parents of an Afghan national coming on Student
visa for higher education (graduation and above) and
research.
8)
Afghan seamen who are discharged at a foreign port for being
returned to India but are signed on at an Indian port and
under the terms of their Articles, required to be returned
for discharge to the port of their engagement.
9)
A Person of Indian Origin, who does not possess an OCI card,
spouse/children of an Indian citizen/Person of Indian Origin/OCI cardholder,
(other than those registered as OCI cardholders).
10)
Members of minority communities in Afghanistan(Hindus & Sikhs),
to visit their friends/relatives, pilgrimage/religious purposes etc.

INSTRUCTIONS
Those travelling to India are also advised to go through
instructions available on the website of Bureau of Immigration at
https://boi.gov.in.
Each online
application form is meant for one person only. Separate
application has to be filed for each applicant.
Furnish information exactly in the manner asked for in the forms, especially the names, passport number, address and date of birth.
Applicants are
required to verify the application details before submitting the
online application. They have an option to save the online
application form, in case it is not to be submitted.
Once the online
application form is submitted by the applicant, then further
modifications are not allowed. Hence applicants are requested to
check and validate the details before submitting the online
application form.
Applicants are requested to keep the Application Id (generated
automatically after the submission of online form) for further
communications. ...
Recent front facing photograph with white background and photo/bio page of Passport and National Identity Card (Tazkira) containing personal details like name, date of birth, nationality, expiry date etc. are to be uploaded mandatorily by the applicant. Additionally one more documents depending upon the visa type would also be required to be uploaded. The application is liable to be rejected if the uploaded documents and photograph are not clear as per the specifications.
The documents required
for completing the Visa application is indicated against each
type of Visa. Please keep all these documents ready at hand
before beginning to fill your Visa application. All documents
including Business cards, Invitation letters etc. must be in
English language, failing which the application would be liable
for rejection.
Applicant should carry a copy of Electronic Travel Authorization
(ETA) along with him/her at the time of travel. Please confirm
that your ETA status is shown as 'GRANTED' on this website prior
to commencement of your journey. Applicants can track the status
of their application online by clicking Visa Status.
Biometric details of
the applicant will be mandatorily captured at Immigration on
arrival in India.
You must travel on the Passport which you have applied for Visa.
Entry will be allowed into India on the new passport even if ETA
has been issued on the old passport, however in such cases a
traveller must carry the old passport on which ETA was issued.
