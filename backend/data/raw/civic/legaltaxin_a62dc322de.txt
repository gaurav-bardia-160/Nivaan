What is a Private Limited Company?
A Private Limited Company is a separate legal entity registered under the Companies Act, 2013. It is owned by a minimum of two shareholders, managed by a minimum of two directors, and offers limited liability, brand recognition and perpetual succession. It is the most preferred business structure for startups and growing businesses in India because it makes raising funds from investors and banks easier.
What is Company Registration in India?
Company registration is the process of incorporating your business with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). Once registered, the company gets a Certificate of Incorporation, a Corporate Identification Number (CIN), PAN and TAN, and exists as a legal entity separate from its owners. A registered company can open a current account in its own name, sign contracts, own property, take loans and apply for government schemes and licences such as GST, MSME and Startup India.
Benefits of Private Limited Company Registration
- Limited liability: Shareholders are liable only up to the unpaid value of their shares, so personal assets stay protected.
- Separate legal entity: The company can own assets, sue and be sued in its own name.
- Easy fundraising: Equity can be issued to investors, angel funds and venture capital firms.
- Perpetual succession: The company continues even if a director or shareholder leaves or passes away.
- Credibility: Customers, banks and vendors trust a registered company more than an unregistered business.
- Tax benefits: Eligible companies can opt for concessional tax regimes and claim business expenses as per the Income Tax Act.
- Brand protection: Your company name is protected under company law, and you can further secure it with trademark registration.

Eligibility and Rules for Private Limited Company Registration
- A minimum of 2 directors and 2 shareholders is required. The same person can be both a director and a shareholder.
- The maximum number of members is limited to 200.
- At least one director must be a resident of India, meaning he or she has stayed in India for 120 days or more in the previous financial year.
- There is no minimum paid-up capital requirement.
- The company must have a registered office address in India, with proof such as a rent agreement or ownership document, and a recent utility bill.
- The right to transfer shares is restricted by the Articles of Association (AOA).
- The company cannot invite the general public to subscribe to its shares.
- The company name must include "Private Limited" and must be unique and not identical or too similar to an existing company or trademark.
How to Register a Private Limited Company in India
The following steps are involved in the online company registration process:
- Obtain Digital Signature Certificate (DSC): All proposed directors and subscribers need a DSC to sign the incorporation forms electronically.
- Apply for company name: Propose up to two names in Part A of the SPICe+ form (or through the RUN service) for approval by the Registrar. The name must comply with the MCA naming guidelines.
- File SPICe+ (INC-32): This single integrated form is used to apply for the Director Identification Number (DIN), incorporation, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC and bank account opening.
- Draft and file e-MOA (INC-33) and e-AOA (INC-34): These documents define the objectives, capital structure and internal rules of the company.
- Pay government fees and stamp duty: Fees depend on the authorised capital and the state of the registered office.
- Receive Certificate of Incorporation: After the ROC verifies the application, it issues the Certificate of Incorporation with your company's CIN, PAN and TAN.
Documents Required for Private Limited Company Registration
Documents Required for Indian Directors and Shareholders
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card (for identity and address proof)
- Voter ID, passport or driving licence (any one as additional proof)
- Latest bank statement or utility bill (not older than 2 months) as residential proof
- Passport size photograph
- Mobile number and email ID
- Specimen signature
Documents Required for Foreign Directors and Shareholders
- Passport (notarised or apostilled)
- Residential proof such as a driving licence, residence card or bank statement (notarised or apostilled)
- Passport size photograph
- Mobile number and email ID
Documents issued outside India must be notarised or apostilled in the country of origin, or attested by the Indian Embassy or Consulate, as applicable.
Documents Required for the Registered Office
- Rent agreement or sale deed or property ownership document
- NOC from the owner of the premises
- Latest electricity, water or telephone bill (not older than 2 months)
Timeline for Private Limited Company Registration
Private Limited Company registration generally takes 15 to 25 days, depending on the readiness of your documents, name availability and the speed of government approval.
- Day 1-3: Document collection, DSC and name selection
- Day 3-7: Name approval from the Registrar
- Day 7-15: Drafting and filing of SPICe+, e-MOA and e-AOA
- Day 15-25: ROC verification and Certificate of Incorporation with CIN, PAN and TAN
The above timeline is indicative. Delays can occur if the ROC raises queries or the documents need correction.

Compliances After Private Limited Company Registration
- Appoint the first statutory auditor within 30 days of incorporation (Form ADT-1)
- Hold the first Board meeting within 30 days of incorporation
- Open the company's current account and deposit the subscribed capital
- Issue share certificates to the subscribers within 60 days of incorporation
- File the Declaration for Commencement of Business (Form INC-20A) within 180 days
- Annual filings: financial statements (AOC-4), annual return (MGT-7) and income tax return
- Annual DIR-3 KYC for every director holding a DIN

Frequently Asked Questions on Private Limited Company Registration
What is a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)?
A DSC is an electronic signature issued by a Certifying Authority. Directors and subscribers use it to sign the incorporation forms filed on the MCA portal.
Is it safe to register a company online?
Yes. Registration is done directly on the official MCA portal. Working with an experienced professional firm like LegalTax helps ensure the documents are correct and the filing is compliant.
How many directors and shareholders are required for a Private Limited Company?
A Private Limited Company needs a minimum of 2 directors and 2 shareholders. The maximum number of members is 200. A director can also be a shareholder, and at least one director must be a resident of India.
Do I need to be physically present for company registration?
No. The process is completely online. Documents are filed electronically and verified through DSC and e-signature, so you do not need to visit any government office.
Is there a minimum capital requirement for a Private Limited Company?
No. There is no minimum paid-up capital requirement under the Companies Act, 2013. You can start with any authorised capital that suits your business, though government fees and stamp duty depend on it.
How long does Private Limited Company registration take?
It generally takes around 15 to 25 days, depending on the readiness of documents, name availability and the speed of government approval.
What are the Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA)?
The MOA defines the company's name, objectives, registered office state and capital. The AOA contains the internal rules, including the roles and powers of directors and shareholders and restrictions on share transfer.
Who can be a shareholder or director of a Private Limited Company?
Any individual aged 18 years or above can be a shareholder. A director must be a natural person with a DIN and a DSC. Indian residents need a PAN card, and foreign nationals need a passport.
Can a salaried person be a director of a Private Limited Company?
Yes, a salaried person can be a director, subject to the terms of their employment contract and any conflict of interest with their employer.
What compliances are required after incorporation?
After incorporation, a company must appoint its first auditor within 30 days, hold the first board meeting within 30 days, open a bank account, issue share certificates, and file the commencement of business declaration (INC-20A) within 180 days. Annual compliances include AOC-4, MGT-7, income tax return and DIR-3 KYC for directors.
