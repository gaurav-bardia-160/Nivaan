About LegalTax.in
We didn’t start in a boardroom.
We started with one person, one laptop, and a problem nobody was solving simply enough.
In 2018, Subhash Chandra Bose started LegalTax alone. No co-founders. No investors. No safety net. Just a stubborn belief that legal, tax, and compliance services in India were broken — too complicated, too expensive, and too inaccessible for the people who needed them most.
Startups were drowning in paperwork before they even had a product. Founders were paying lakhs for basic compliance they didn’t understand. And everyday people had no idea where to even begin.
So he built the solution he wished existed.
One client at a time. One problem at a time.
Today, LegalTax is a full-stack legal and business services platform — helping startups, entrepreneurs, and growing businesses navigate law, tax, compliance, and digital growth — all under one roof.
Our Mission
To make legal and financial services simple, affordable, and accessible — for every founder, every startup, every dreamer building something real.
No jargon. No unnecessary complexity. No gatekeeping.
Just clear guidance, honest advice, and execution that actually gets done.
Our Vision
A future where no startup fails because of legal or compliance gaps.
We believe the best ideas deserve the best foundation. Whether you’re registering your first company, filing your first GST return, protecting your first trademark, or scaling your digital presence — LegalTax is built to be the partner that grows with you.
From day one to IPO.
The Founder
Subhash Chandra Bose — Founder, LegalTax
There’s a certain kind of person who sees a broken system and decides to fix it instead of complaining about it.
Mr. Subhash is that person.
In 2018, with no backing, no team, and no guarantees — he started LegalTax from scratch. What kept him going wasn’t funding or fanfare. It was founders reaching out at midnight confused about their company structure. It was small business owners who’d been overcharged and underserved for years. It was the quiet satisfaction of making something complicated — finally feel simple.
He built LegalTax the hard way. Which is also the right way.
Every system, every service, every process at LegalTax carries that DNA — built by someone who knows what it means to figure things out alone, and decided nobody else should have to.
