The Passport Seva Programme (PSP) Division of the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India, is responsible for Passport services through the Central Passport Organization (CPO) and its network of 37 Passport Offices, 90+ Passport Seva Kendras (PSKs); and 450+ Post Office Passport Seva Kendras (POPSKs). Consular, Passport and Visa services to overseas Indians/Foreign nationals are provided by the PSP Division through the Indian Missions and Posts abroad.
For Passport related services, go to : www.passportindia.gov.in/psp
