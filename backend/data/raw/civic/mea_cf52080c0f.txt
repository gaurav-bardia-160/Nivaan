Visa facility for Indian Nationals (Ordinary Passport Holders)
Foreign Countries/Regions which allow
(i) Travel (Medical) Insurance, (ii) A valid residence permit or valid visa issued by the EU and Schengen member states, the USA, Australia, New Zealand, the Republic of Korea, the UK, Canada, the Russian Federation, or Japan, or a valid residence permit issued by the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain or Oman.
Or
(i) Travel (Medical) Insurance, (ii) Air ticket reservation (with return**), (iii) Invitation provided by a party interested in the applicant’s visit to the Republic of Armenia (local tourist agency, university, employer, other local companies, and entities), and (iv) Proof of financial means (bank statement for the last 3 months, certificate of employment with salary, work contract, other proofs)
** A one-way air ticket is sufficient for individuals arriving for educational or work purposes or under other justified cases.
Alternatively, Indian citizens (holding ordinary passport) may also obtain a Visa on Arrival at the Addis Ababa Bole International Airport by presenting a valid passport with at least six months' validity and paying the applicable visa fee.
