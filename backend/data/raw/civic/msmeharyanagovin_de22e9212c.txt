TITLE: Directorate of MSME, Haryana - *Haryana MSME Samvad 3rd Edition by ASSOCHEM at Leela Ambience Hotel, Gurugram on 23.06.23* MSME Samvad 3rd Edition held today for the Units operating in Haryana by ASSOCHEM. The event was graced by Hon’ble Deputy Chief Minister Sh Dushyant Chautala joined virtually.Sh Anand Mohan Sharan, IAS, Additional Chief Secretary to Govt of Haryana, Industries & Commerce Department was Guest of Honour. Hon’ble Dy CM briefed the initiatives taken and the vision of Govt for MSMEs in Haryana. The event was organised by ASSOCHEM taking stakeholders onboard for the benefits of MSMEs.Worthy ACS touched upon the flagship schemes run by the department. Other dignitaries from associated fields also shared their vision and how the MSMEs can be benefited.Sh Shashi Kant, Additional Director, MSME Directorate, GoH informed house about the steps being undertaken for the benefits of Units.
