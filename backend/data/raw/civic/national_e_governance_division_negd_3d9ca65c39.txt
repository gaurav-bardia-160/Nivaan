TITLE: National e-Governance Division (NeGD) - Subpage

As part of its continued outreach activities, the National e-Governance Division (NeGD), Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India, in collaboration with the North Eastern Regional Institute of Science and Technology (NERIST) and the Institution of Engineers (India) (IEI), Arunachal Pradesh State Centre, organised a Digital India Talk Show cum Interactive Workshop at the NERIST Seminar Hall, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, on June 19, 2026. The workshop aimed at creating awareness about the Government of India’s flagship Digital India initiatives, promoting digital literacy, and familiarising participants with citizen-centric digital services and emerging technologies that are transforming governance and public service delivery.
