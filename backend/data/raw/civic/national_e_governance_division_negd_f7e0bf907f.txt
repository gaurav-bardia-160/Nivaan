Vyay-Niyam Sahayak (व्यय-नियम सहायक) is a conversational AI assistant developed by the National e-Governance Division (NeGD), MeitY. It offers officers, vendors and citizens a single, reliable place to get answers on the Ministry of Finance’s published financial and procurement rules.
The assistant responds to queries on the General Financial Rules (GFR), 2017, which set out the procedures and propriety governing public spending; the Delegation of Financial Powers Rules (DFPR), 2024, which define the limits of each authority’s sanctioning powers; and the Manuals for Procurement of Goods, Works and Consultancy & Other Services, which guide transparent and efficient public procurement. Every answer cites the specific rule, annexure or Office Memorandum behind it, so users can verify it at the source.
