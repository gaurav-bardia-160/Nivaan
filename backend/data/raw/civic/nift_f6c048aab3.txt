The National Institute of Fashion Technology Entrance Examination (NIFTEE) 2026 is a national-level test conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission into all UG, PG, Ph.D. and special category programs of the National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) for the 2026–27 academic session for B.Des., B.F.Tech., NLEA (Lateral Entry) for UG, M.Des., M.F.M., M.F.Tech., Artisans Category, International/NRI category, and Ph.D. programs.
NTA conducts the entrance exam, displays answer key, processes challenges, and declares results.
