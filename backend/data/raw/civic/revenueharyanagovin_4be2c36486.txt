TITLE: Revenue and Disaster Management Haryana

The Department of Revenue and Disaster Management plays a pivotal role in maintenance of land records, revenue collection and coordination of relief efforts during natural calamities.
Revenue & Disaster Management System ensures that administrative units at the district and sub-district levels function smoothly and in alignment with state policies. Through modernization and digital initiatives, the department aims to bring transparency and efficiency in service delivery to citizens.
