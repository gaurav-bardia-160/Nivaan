About DIC Sehore
DISTRICT INFORMATICS CENTRE (NIC-Sehore) was setup in 1989 in accordance with the M.O.U. signed between NIC and Govt. of Madhya Pradesh to set up NICNET facilities in M.P.National Informatics Centre (NIC) Sehore is a premier Science & Technology institution of the Government of India, providing ICT solutions adopting best practices, integrated services and technical solutions Sehore in government departments. Since its inception NIC Sehore is providing required support services in design, development, and implementation of appropriate MIS/Databases, Training, and Electronic Communication and processing of various Departmental databases. NIC District Centre provides common ICT Infrastructure and support services to government departments with Products and Solutions designed to adopting e-Governance initiatives. NIC has taken various e-Governance activities like Samagra Samajik Surksha Monitoring , e-Uparjan, e-Scholarship, e-District (Lok Seva kendra), National Animal Disease Reporting System (NADRS), Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MG-NREGA), Land Record Computerisation-Bhuabhilekh & Bhunaksha, Election MIS, Polling Party Formation for State Legislative Assembly & Parliament elections, Samadhan Online, NICNET and NKN network services, Local Area Network at Collectorate premises and Video Conferencing facility has been appreciated. The video conferencing facility extended up to five blocks and Tehsils. The services of NIC District Centre, Sehore has become an indispensable tool for the district administration and various other state and central government departments.
