STQC's Citizen /Client Charter
The Citizen /Client Charter embodies the commitment of the STQC Directorate, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, to introduce a greater measure of transparency, responsiveness and accountability in the functioning of the Directorate. The STQC Directorate is committed to provide its services in the fields of Testing, Calibration, Certification, IT and e-Governance, training and guidance to Information Technology, Telecommunications and Electronics Industry etc. STQC Directorate in the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology is responsible for dissemination of the above mentioned services globally.
