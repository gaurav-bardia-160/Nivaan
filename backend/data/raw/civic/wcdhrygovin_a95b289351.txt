TITLE: Vision | Women and Child Development Department, Haryana | India

Vision
To facilitate the economic and social empowerment of women, enabling them to contribute as healthy, confident, and equal partners in society within an environment free from violence and discrimination. To provide children and adolescent girls with opportunities for holistic growth and development in a safe, secure, and nurturing environment.
