Commercial Taxes Department, Government of Karnataka (ctax.kar.nic.in), oversees the administration of revenue taxes across Karnataka. It delivers services including e-Way Bill monitoring, e-Sugam for non-GST goods, input tax credit reconciliations, dealer audits, and taxpayer facilitation desks for trade and industry.
