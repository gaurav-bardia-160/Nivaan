MP e-Khanij Portal is the official digital mining administration platform of the Mineral Resources Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. It governs mining lease grants, electronic transit passes (e-TP), royalty calculations, weighbridge automation, mineral transport vehicle tracking, and illegal mining surveillance to ensure lawful extraction of major and minor minerals.
