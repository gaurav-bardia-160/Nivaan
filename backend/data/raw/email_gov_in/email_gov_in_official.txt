TITLE: National e-Mail Service (NIC Email)
FETCHED_AT: 2026-10-28
---
The National e-Mail Service (email.gov.in) is the sovereign, encrypted government messaging infrastructure developed and managed by the National Informatics Centre (NIC) for all Union and State government officers. Secured through two-factor authentication via the Kavach authentication application, geolocation IP geofencing, and strict data residency within government data centres, it handles millions of official communications daily. Citizen correspondence with departmental officers reaches jurisdictional email.gov.in inboxes, ensuring secure audit trails.
