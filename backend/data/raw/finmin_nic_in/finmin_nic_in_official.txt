---
The Ministry of Finance is the apex union ministry responsible for India's macroeconomic management, taxation, financial legislation, financial institutions, capital markets, and union budget preparation. It comprises six departments: Economic Affairs, Expenditure, Revenue, Financial Services, Public Enterprises, and Investment & Public Asset Management (DIPAM). The official portal provides budget documents, economic surveys, tax notifications, and banking directives.
