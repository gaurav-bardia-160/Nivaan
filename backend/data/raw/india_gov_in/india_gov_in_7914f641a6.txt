Agriculture, Rural & Environment
Agriculture is central to India's economy, particularly in rural areas where it enhances GDP and livelihoods. This sector employs sustainable practices such as soil conservation and promotes biodiversity through initiatives like the green, white, yellow, and blue revolutions. It encompasses produce, resources, research, marketing, animal husbandry, fisheries, and environmental management.

Agricultural Produce
Agriculture is crucial for India's rural economy, significantly impacting GDP through sustainable practices in food security, job creation, and environmental conservation.
