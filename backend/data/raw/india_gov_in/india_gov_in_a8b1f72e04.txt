Youth sports & Culture
The Youth, Sports, and Culture section provides comprehensive details on initiatives for India's youth, focusing on development through sports, cultural engagement, and government support. It covers schemes, welfare programmes, sports infrastructure, and cultural events, reflecting India’s diverse cultural heritage and youthful demographic. This section covers games and sports infrastructure, youth affairs, fairs and festivals, arts and crafts, and heritage and monuments.
Subcategories
Youth Affairs
India empowers its youth through skill development, leadership training, and cultural exchanges, promoting active participation in sports and cultural activities for holistic growth.
