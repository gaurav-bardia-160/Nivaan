NIC Karnataka State Centre (kar.nic.in) leads the architecture and deployment of mission-mode e-governance systems in Karnataka, including Bhoomi (land records), Kaveri (property registration), Khajane (treasury management), Seva Sindhu (citizen services), and Panchatantra (Gram Panchayat MIS), driving digital public services across the state.
