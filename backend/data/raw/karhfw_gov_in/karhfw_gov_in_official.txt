Department of Health and Family Welfare Services, Government of Karnataka, delivers primary, secondary, and tertiary public healthcare services. It administers the Ayushman Bharat - Arogya Karnataka cashless health insurance scheme, maternal and child healthcare, immunization programs, blood bank directories, and state healthcare infrastructure.
