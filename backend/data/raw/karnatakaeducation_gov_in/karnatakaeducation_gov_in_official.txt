Department of School Education and Literacy, Karnataka, administers school education policies, curriculum, and examinations (KSEAB). Through the Student Achievement Tracking System (SATS), every student in Karnataka is assigned a unique enrollment number tracking academic progress, attendance, mid-day meal benefits, and scholarship distributions.
