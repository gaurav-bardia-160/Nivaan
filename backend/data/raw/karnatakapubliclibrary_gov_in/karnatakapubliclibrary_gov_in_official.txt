Department of Public Libraries, Karnataka, manages the network of state central, district central, and taluk libraries. The digital portal allows citizens to access thousands of e-books, research journals, competitive exam preparation resources, and rare historical manuscripts in Kannada, English, and regional languages through a unified digital library membership.
