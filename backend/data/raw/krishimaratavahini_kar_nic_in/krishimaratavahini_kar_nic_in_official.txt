TITLE: Krishi Marata Vahini - APMC Market Rates Karnataka
FETCHED_AT: 2026-10-28
---
Krishi Marata Vahini, operated by the Karnataka State Agricultural Marketing Board (KSAMB), publishes daily commodity arrivals, minimum, maximum, and modal market prices across all APMC (Agricultural Produce Market Committee) mandis in Karnataka. Farmers can track live price trends to plan the sale of cereals, pulses, oilseeds, fruits, and vegetables.
