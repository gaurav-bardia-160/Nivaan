Department of Labour, Government of Karnataka, protects the rights and welfare of organized and unorganized workers. Through the Karnataka Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (KBOCWWB) and Karmika portal, workers can register for welfare benefits, educational scholarships, maternity assistance, pension schemes, and accident relief.
