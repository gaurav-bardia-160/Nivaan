Madhya Pradesh Bhulekh (landrecords.mp.gov.in / mpbhulekh.gov.in), under the Revenue Department of Madhya Pradesh, provides computerized land ownership documents. Farmers and landholders can search and download certified copies of Khasra (plot record), Khatauni (record of rights - ROR), B1 Naksha (cadastral map), diverted land orders, and encumbrance certificates for agricultural and commercial parcels.
