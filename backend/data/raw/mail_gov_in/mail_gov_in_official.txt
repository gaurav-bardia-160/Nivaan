TITLE: Government of India Webmail (mail.gov.in)
FETCHED_AT: 2026-10-28
---
Government of India Webmail (mail.gov.in) is the unified secure gateway providing web-based email access to civil servants and public officials across India. Integrated with government personnel directories and LDAP, it enforces TLS 1.3 encryption, anti-phishing protection, and automated virus scanning to prevent data leaks. Public queries and administrative submissions routed to designated departmental nodal officers are processed through official mail.gov.in endpoints.
