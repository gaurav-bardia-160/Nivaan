TITLE: National Knowledge Network (NKN)
FETCHED_AT: 2026-10-28
---
National Knowledge Network (NKN) is India's multi-gigabit nationwide communications backbone connecting academic, scientific, and research institutions. Managed by the National Informatics Centre (NIC), NKN interconnects IITs, IISc, CSIR laboratories, agricultural universities, and supercomputing grids with high-speed, secure optical networks, enabling collaborative research, telemedicine, virtual classrooms, and large-scale scientific data sharing.
