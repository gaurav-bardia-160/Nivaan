2.  Applicant is allowed to fill basic details in the Application form.

3.  On submission of the application, it will redirect to payment gateway. On successful payment the Aadhaar authentication will take place.

4.  If the payment transaction failed due to any unknown reasons, applicant may again initiate for payment using the option "Regenerate online PAN application".

5.  The Aadhaar will be authenticated on real time basis from UIDAI server. If the authentication status is found to be perfect match then the application will be      processed further.

6.  In case of failure of Aadhaar Authentication, Auto Refund will be processed for the Application. Applicant can submit the new application in physical mode.

8.  After successful authentication of OTP, applicant address details will be fetched from the UIDAI and populated in the PAN form.

9.  In case the e-KYC feature fails due to any unknown technical reasons or the e-KYC feature has not worked successfully you may use the option "Regenerate online      PAN application" to complete the e-KYC Process.

11. After successful completion of e-KYC process, the application data has to verified by the applicant and provide other necessary information and submit.

12. The applicant cannot change the data after Aadhaar authentication and after successfully processed through eKYC mode.
