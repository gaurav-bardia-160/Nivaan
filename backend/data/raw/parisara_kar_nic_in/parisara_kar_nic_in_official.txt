TITLE: Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB)
FETCHED_AT: 2026-10-28
---
Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB) implements environmental protection statutes across Karnataka. Through its online consent management system (OCMMS / Parisara), industrial units apply for Consent for Establishment (CFE) and Consent for Operation (CFO), submit environmental statements, and monitor air and water quality compliance.
