Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MP ESB, formerly MP Professional Examination Board - PEB), conducts competitive examinations for recruitment to state government posts and professional course admissions (e.g. Police Constable, Patwari, Sub-Engineer, Teacher Eligibility Test - TET). The portal delivers exam notifications, admit cards, answer keys, objection tracking, and normalized merit lists.
