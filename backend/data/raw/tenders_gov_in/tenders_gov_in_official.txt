Government of India Tenders Portal (tenders.gov.in, transitioning to the CPPP e-Publishing System) provides centralized access to tender notices, tender documents, corrigenda, and contract award notifications issued by public bodies. Vendors can search active bids by organization, classification, tender value, sub-department, and geographical location.
