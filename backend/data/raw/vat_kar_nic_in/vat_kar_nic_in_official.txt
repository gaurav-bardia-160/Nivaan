Commercial Taxes Department of Karnataka (formerly vat.kar.nic.in, now integrated with gstkarnataka.gov.in) administers state taxes, including Karnataka Goods and Services Tax (KGST), Profession Tax (KPT), and non-GST commodity taxes. The portal provides online registration, tax return submissions, electronic payment facilities, and tax clearance certifications.
