The Wildlife Wing of the Department of Forests and Wildlife, Government of Haryana, protects wildlife sanctuaries (including Sultanpur National Park and Kalesar National Park), manages animal rescue operations, prevents poaching under the Wildlife Protection Act, and promotes eco-tourism initiatives.
