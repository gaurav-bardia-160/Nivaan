# Vision and Mission
Vision
To empower Aadhaar number holders of India with a unique identity and a digital platform to authenticate anytime, anywhere.
Mission
  * ![arrow](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowBullet.svg)
To provide for good governance, efficient, transparent and targeted delivery of subsidies, benefits and services, the expenditure for which is incurred from the Consolidated Fund of India or the Consolidated Fund of State to Aadhaar number holders of India through assigning of unique identity numbers.
  * ![arrow](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowBullet.svg)
To develop policy, procedure and system for issuing Aadhaar number to individuals seeking to enrol of India, who request for same by submitting their demographic information and biometric information by undergoing the process of enrolment.
  * ![arrow](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowBullet.svg)
To develop policy, procedure and systems for Aadhaar number holders for updating and authenticating their digital identity.
  * ![arrow](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowBullet.svg)
Ensure availability, scalability and resilience of the technology infrastructure.
  * ![arrow](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowBullet.svg)
Build a long term sustainable organization to carry forward the vision and values of the UIDAI.
  * ![arrow](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowBullet.svg)
To ensure security and confidentiality of identity information and authentication records of the Aadhaar number holders.
  * ![arrow](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowBullet.svg)
To ensure compliance of Aadhaar Act by all individual and agencies in letter and spirit.
  * ![arrow](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowBullet.svg)
To make regulations & rules consistent with the Aadhaar Act, for carrying out the provisions of the Aadhaar Act.

Frequently Asked Questions
### 
How to download the Aadhaar app?
You can download the Aadhaar app on your Android/iOS device from the Play Store/App Store. For Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.uidai.pehchaan For iOS: https://apps.apple.com/in/app/aadhaar/id6744029871 
### 
How to register on the Aadhaar app?
After downloading the app, the Aadhaar Number Holder (ANH): →Select Language→ Enters their Aadhaar number → Agrees to the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy → Chooses the SIM card → Performs Face Authentication → Gets registered on the app → Sets a 6-digit PIN to secure access to the app.
### 
Which services are available on this app?
Presently, the Aadhaar app offers various services like - Mobile Number Update, Address Update, Biometric lock/unlock, Selective Sharing of Aadhaar Verifiable Credential, check Authentication History, download e-Aadhaar, share Contact Card, Track my request, etc.
