# Our Offices
The Unique Identification Authority of India operates through a well-structured framework of specialised divisions that manage enrolment, authentication, technology, security, finance, legal matters, and public outreach. These functions work in coordination to ensure seamless service delivery, robust data protection, efficient operations, and continuous innovation in Aadhaar services across the country.

Head Office
![Head_office.webp](https://backend.uidai.gov.in/get/files/media/document/2026-06/Head_office.webp)
At the Head Office of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDIA), the CEO is supported by eight Deputy Directors General (DDGs), who are Joint Secretary–level officers overseeing key functional areas. They are assisted by a structured team including ADGs, Deputy Directors, Section Officers, and support staff. With a sanctioned strength of 134, the HO ensures effective coordination, administration, and policy implementation.
Know More![Know More](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowLeftBlue.svg)
Regional Offices
![ro.webp](https://backend.uidai.gov.in/get/files/media/document/2026-06/ro.webp)
Each Regional Office of the Unique Identification Authority of India is led by a Deputy Director General (DDG), ensuring effective regional administration and service delivery. The DDG is supported by a well-defined team comprising Directors, Assistant Directors General, Deputy Directors, Section Officers, and other administrative staff, enabling smooth operations, coordination, and implementation of Aadhaar-related activities across their respective jurisdictions.
Know More![Know More](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowLeftBlue.svg)
State Offices
![state_offices.webp](https://backend.uidai.gov.in/get/files/media/document/2026-06/state_offices.webp)
State Offices support Aadhaar-related operations and coordinate activities within their assigned jurisdictions. Each office is responsible for overseeing implementation, stakeholder coordination, and service delivery across designated states and regions, ensuring efficient administration and accessibility of Aadhaar services throughout the country.
Know More![Know More](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowLeftBlue.svg)
Technology Centre
![Tech_center.webp](https://backend.uidai.gov.in/get/files/media/document/2026-06/Tech_center.webp)
The Technology Centre of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) is responsible for managing Aadhaar’s IT infrastructure, data security, and system operations. It ensures reliable authentication services, maintains databases, and supports technological innovation for seamless delivery of Aadhaar services nationwide.

Frequently Asked Questions
### 
How to download the Aadhaar app?
You can download the Aadhaar app on your Android/iOS device from the Play Store/App Store. For Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.uidai.pehchaan For iOS: https://apps.apple.com/in/app/aadhaar/id6744029871 
### 
How to register on the Aadhaar app?
After downloading the app, the Aadhaar Number Holder (ANH): →Select Language→ Enters their Aadhaar number → Agrees to the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy → Chooses the SIM card → Performs Face Authentication → Gets registered on the app → Sets a 6-digit PIN to secure access to the app.
### 
Which services are available on this app?
Presently, the Aadhaar app offers various services like - Mobile Number Update, Address Update, Biometric lock/unlock, Selective Sharing of Aadhaar Verifiable Credential, check Authentication History, download e-Aadhaar, share Contact Card, Track my request, etc.
