# Building the Future of Digital Identity
The Scheme for Innovation and Technology Association with Aadhaar (SITAA) is a strategic initiative by UIDAI to foster a culture of open innovation and co-creation around India’s Identity Technology (ID Tech) ecosystem.   
  
**Vision** – “To strengthen India’s digital identity ecosystem by fostering innovation and promoting indigenization in identity technologies.”
# About SITAA
**SITAA at a Glance**  
  
A national innovation and collaboration initiative by UIDAI to strengthen India’s digital identity ecosystem through research, technology development, and ecosystem partnerships.

# Who is SITAA for
Designed for Innovators Across the Ecosystem
![Startups](https://backend.uidai.gov.in/get/files/media/document/2026-06/plane_0.svg)
# Startups
Early and growth-stage startups developing innovative solutions in the digital identity domain, such as authentication, biometrics, identity verification, fraud prevention, privacy enhancement, digital trust, and emerging identity technologies
![Academia & Research](https://backend.uidai.gov.in/get/files/media/document/2026-06/hat_0.svg)
# Academia & Research
Universities and research institutions working on AI, privacy, biometrics, cybersecurity, and inclusive digital systems
![Industry & Providers](https://backend.uidai.gov.in/get/files/media/document/2026-06/building_0.svg)
# Industry & Providers
Established firms and technology providers building mature ID solutions for Aadhaar ecosystem requirements.
