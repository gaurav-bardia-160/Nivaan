About your Aadhaar
Know about Aadhaar, a unique 12-digit identity number issued by UIDAI to residents of India through a free enrolment process.
Read More![Read More](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowLeftBlue.svg)
Features of Aadhaar
Features of Aadhaar include uniqueness, portability, secure authentication, scalable technology and a random identity number.
Read More![Read More](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowLeftBlue.svg)
Usage of Aadhaar
Usage of Aadhaar enables secure identity verification, direct benefit transfer, transparency and seamless access to services nationwide.
Read More![Read More](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowLeftBlue.svg)
Aadhaar Enrolment
Aadhaar enrolment includes form submission, biometric capture, document verification and issuance of Enrolment ID slip.
Read More![Read More](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowLeftBlue.svg)
Aadhaar Generation
Aadhaar generation includes quality checks, validations and de-duplication to ensure secure and unique Aadhaar issuance.
Read More![Read More](https://uidai.gov.in/assets/svgs/icons/ArrowLeftBlue.svg)
Updating Data on Aadhaar
Update Aadhaar demographic and biometric details online or at enrolment centres to keep your Aadhaar accurate and up to date.

Frequently Asked Questions
### 
How to download the Aadhaar app?
You can download the Aadhaar app on your Android/iOS device from the Play Store/App Store. For Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.uidai.pehchaan For iOS: https://apps.apple.com/in/app/aadhaar/id6744029871 
### 
How to register on the Aadhaar app?
After downloading the app, the Aadhaar Number Holder (ANH): →Select Language→ Enters their Aadhaar number → Agrees to the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy → Chooses the SIM card → Performs Face Authentication → Gets registered on the app → Sets a 6-digit PIN to secure access to the app.
### 
Which services are available on this app?
Presently, the Aadhaar app offers various services like - Mobile Number Update, Address Update, Biometric lock/unlock, Selective Sharing of Aadhaar Verifiable Credential, check Authentication History, download e-Aadhaar, share Contact Card, Track my request, etc.
