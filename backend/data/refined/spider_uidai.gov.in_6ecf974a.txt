# Offline Verification Seeking Entity(OVSE)
Offline verification refers to the framework that enables entities to perform Offline Verification of Aadhaar number holder for identity verification without connecting to the UIDAI’s CIDR (Central Identity Data Repository) . It allows secure, consent-based, and offline identity verification.
Prominent Use Cases for Entities for Offline Verification
1. Hospitality and Tourism  
2. Healthcare  
3. Gig Economy  
4. Entertainment & Public Events  
5. Real Estate and Housing  
6. Education  
7. Recruitment
8. Facility Management  
9. NGO and Trusts  
10. Central and State Government departments  
11. Fin tech Companies  
12. Pilgrims Verification  
13. Social/Community/Matchmaking  
14. Or any other entity requiring Aadhaar offline verification
Technical Requirement to become an OVSE
  1. Logo in SVG format (<10Kb)*
  2. A call back URL where UIDAI will post the response data*
  3. Organization Name to be displayed in Aadhaar App*
  4. Domain Name for verification*
  5. Class 3 or 2, Public Certificate with 2 Year Validity*
  6. Application ID - Android/iOS (App to App intent call)#

* Mandatory Requirements   
# Conditional Requirement (mandatory only in app-to-app intent cal)

Frequently Asked Questions
### 
How can an entity become a registered OVSE?
An entity willing to became OVSE may submit the application through https://ovse.uidai.gov.in/. UIDAI may register the entity as a registered OVSE, after verification of the submitted details.
### 
Can registered OVSE collect and store my verifiable credentials?
Yes. A registered OVSE may collect and store verifiable credentials shared with the explicit consent of the Aadhaar Number Holder.
### 
Can offline verification be performed on behalf of another entity?
No. Offline verification cannot be performed on behalf of another entity.
