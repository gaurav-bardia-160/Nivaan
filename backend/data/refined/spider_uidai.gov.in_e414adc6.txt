What is Aadhaar Seva Kendra (ASK)?
'Aadhaar Seva Kendra' or ASK is a single-stop destination for all Aadhaar services in a comfortable air-conditioned facility. All ASKs are wheelchair friendly and have special provisions to service the elderly and specially abled. More info on ASKs is available here: [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in).
