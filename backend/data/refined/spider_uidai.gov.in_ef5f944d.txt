# Training, Testing and Certification Documents
UIDAI provides comprehensive training, testing and certification programs to ensure enrolment staff maintain quality and uniformity in Aadhaar enrolment and update processes. Certification is mandatory for roles such as Enrolment Supervisor/Operator and Child Enrolment Lite Client Operator.
