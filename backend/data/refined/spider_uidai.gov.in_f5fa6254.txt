## Services that require mobile number to be registered with Aadhaar
![Service Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/DownloadAadhaar.a2d1e98fb6b4bea18da11eb69d7ee5e6.svg)
### Download Aadhaar
Click here to download digitally signed and password protected electronic copy of the Aadhaar.
![Arrow Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/arrowRight.36a2fb68edf604ae8a0b73169525cdb3.svg)
![Service Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/RetrieveUid.71a1dc8b0f3ae94e8c910d348bf5459c.svg)
### Retrieve Aadhaar number/EID/SID
Click here to find out Aadhaar number, Enrolment ID (EID) or Service ID (SID).
![Arrow Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/arrowRight.36a2fb68edf604ae8a0b73169525cdb3.svg)
![Service Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/VerifyMobile.9f06273a26fced1586176233a824a873.svg)
### Verify email / mobile
Click here to verify mobile and email registered with the Aadhaar.
![Arrow Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/arrowRight.36a2fb68edf604ae8a0b73169525cdb3.svg)
![Service Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/Death-icon.727b9fb544db4c5f6912fc85fbf44012.svg)
### Report death of a family member 
Click here to report death of a Family Member for his/her Aadhaar deactivation.
![Arrow Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/arrowRight.36a2fb68edf604ae8a0b73169525cdb3.svg)
![Service Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/DocumentUpdate.367e861345889bdb4d30906dad6b698a.svg)
### Document update
Click Here to upload your Proof of Identity (PoI) and Proof of Address (PoA) Documents.
**This service is free of cost till 14/06/2027.**
![Arrow Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/arrowRight.36a2fb68edf604ae8a0b73169525cdb3.svg)
![Service Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/GenerateVirtualId.99091915b8c8ab9d474e40098f4ce04f.svg)
### VID generator
Click here to generate 16 Digit Virtual ID(VID) linked to your Aadhaar.
![Arrow Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/arrowRight.36a2fb68edf604ae8a0b73169525cdb3.svg)
![Service Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/AadhaarLockUnlock.1829e87a7682bc7753b7c8c8c8727d28.svg)
### Lock / Unlock Aadhaar
Click here to temporarily lock/unlock your Aadhaar. Please use this service carefully.
![Arrow Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/arrowRight.36a2fb68edf604ae8a0b73169525cdb3.svg)
![Service Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/BankLinking.2f5998ed8452667406cb93aa5298209b.svg)
### Bank seeding status
Click here to find your Bank Seeding Status.
![Arrow Icon](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/static/media/arrowRight.36a2fb68edf604ae8a0b73169525cdb3.svg)
