# GovBank Assistant - Terms of Use & Legal Disclaimers

**Last Verified:** October 2026

---

## 1. Nature of Service

GovBank Assistant is an informational procedural assistant created to help citizens navigate official Indian government portals and civic procedures (Aadhaar enrollment, PAN applications, Income Tax Return filing, consumer court complaints, loan agreements, and welfare schemes).

**Critical Notice:**
> **GovBank Assistant is NOT an official government authority, a legal firm, an authorized tax practitioner, or a chartered accountant.**  
> The information provided is a plain-language summary designed to facilitate navigation. It does not constitute legal, tax, or financial advice.

---

## 2. Official Source Provenance

All procedural answers return an official citation, verification date, and direct hyperlink to the authoritative portal:
- **Income Tax Department:** `https://www.incometax.gov.in/`
- **Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI):** `https://uidai.gov.in/`
- **National Securities Depository Limited (Protean/NSDL PAN):** `https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/`
- **Passport Seva Portal:** `https://www.passportindia.gov.in/`
- **Bureau of Immigration (e-FRRO):** `https://indianfrro.gov.in/`
- **Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (Parivahan):** `https://parivahan.gov.in/`
- **Reserve Bank of India (RBI):** `https://www.rbi.org.in/`
- **National Portal of India:** `https://www.india.gov.in/`

Users are strongly urged to verify all deadlines, fees, and form requirements directly on the respective official portal prior to making payments or legal submissions.
